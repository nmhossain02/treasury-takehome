# Demo images

The app's **Try a sample** chooser bundles five public COLA label sets that exercise real local OCR, automatic record matching, and label checks:

- Seven Fathoms premium rum (2 photos)
- Turtle Rabbit tequila (2 photos)
- Dark Arts straight bourbon whisky (2 photos)
- Muralist straight bourbon whisky (3 photos)
- Crown Royal Canadian whisky (2 photos)

The Seven Fathoms images remain at the root for convenient manual upload. The other sets are under `samples/`.

These are public registry images retrieved from the TTB Public COLA Registry. Provenance and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `../public-cola/manifest.json`. The label artwork may contain third-party intellectual property; it is included only as a prototype evaluation fixture, and no public-domain claim is made.
